NEON HANDS

Gesture Synth

An original hand-tracked instrument. Use your left hand to pick a scale degree and mode, and your right hand to shape the chord, octave, filter and volume — all in real time, no keyboard required.

How to play

Hold both hands up where the camera can see them. Everything is driven by simple gestures — no clicking needed once you start.

Left hand — melody & mode

  • Number of fingers up (1–5) picks the scale degree of the chord root.
  • Tilting your left hand inward leans the scale major; outward leans it minor.

Right hand — chord & expression

  • Fingers up (1–4) choose the chord voicing: root triad, 1st inversion, added 7th, or dominant/diminished 7th.
  • Thumb tucked in vs. out shifts the octave up or down.
  • Tilting the right hand changes the filter's brightness.
  • Raising or lowering your right hand controls volume.

Pick a key and synth voice from the top bar any time. This is an original build inspired by gesture-controlled instruments — built from scratch, not a copy of any existing app.
